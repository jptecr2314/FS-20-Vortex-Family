# FS-20 Vortex Family 0.7.1

Fixes the bright patches at the FS-20NA wing roots. T and NA now bind the selected wing atlas after the game's native livery assignment. The NA also keeps the same selected paint on matching albedo and damage slots.

At the most distant LOD, the extended wing and fuselage use the same simplified paint finish. Camouflage and faction markings remain on the textured near and intermediate levels.

All 12 paints and asynchronous loading carry forward from 0.7.0. Install the full runtime package, including BuiltinPaints. Blueprinter 2.1.1 remains required. The creator kit is a separate optional download. Use matching 0.7.1 builds for experimental multiplayer.
