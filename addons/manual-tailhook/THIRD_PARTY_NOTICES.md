# Third-party notices

Nuclear Option is developed by Shockfront Studios. This unofficial plugin is
not affiliated with or endorsed by the studio. A licensed game is required.
No game assemblies, extracted assets or game data are included.

Runtime prerequisites, installed separately: BepInEx 5 and its Harmony library.
The source is compiled against the user's installed Unity/game assemblies.
The optional FS-20C adapter uses the Vortex Family plugin when installed.
Neither Blueprinter nor Vortex Family is required for native aircraft hooks.

The Microsoft.Net.Compilers.Toolset compiler is restored separately from NuGet
by the repository's pinned restore script. No compiler or dependency binaries
are included in this download. Dependency licenses remain with their projects.
